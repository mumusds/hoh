### Design corrections

- **DC-M4T09a** (round 1, high 100 and mediums 003/102): the host's public drain never ends on a diagnostic; only DB_BUSY
  backpressures; low space and non-retryable errors write an inline gap and drop the body; the native reader keeps
  reading and cancel stays reachable.
- **DC-M4T09b** (redaction leaked across chunk boundaries twice: 005, then 200/304): a block's unterminated tail is
  withheld until its newline, completion or the cap; batch and streaming share one redaction function; a property test
  over deterministic splits and flush points asserts published text = redact(full text).
- **DC-M4T09c** (gap records lacked identity twice: 201, then 500/402): `public::gap` is the only constructor of a
  `public.gap` payload, with the full native identity, enforced by a source guard; the process-end gap is written in
  the Attempt end transaction.
