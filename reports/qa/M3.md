Commits:
- "M3-T05 fix: M3L-100 CR-040 draft, skip known oversized frames the adapter does not need (REQ-027)"
- "M3-T05 fix: M3L-100 skip a known frame over the limit to its LF, bounded (REQ-027)"
- "M3-T05: approve CR-040 and relock baseline rev 29 (REQ-027)"

How the fix works:
- A per-adapter rule recognises an oversized frame by its exact byte prefix in the first 4 KiB.
- A recognised frame is discarded read by read up to its LF, so the buffer stays within limit + one read chunk.
- The skip is recorded as an `adapter.diagnostic` (type, length, sha256, head).
- Unknown frames, frames truncated before their LF and frames over 256 MiB still fail with PROTOCOL_FRAME_TOO_LARGE. AC-027-03 is unchanged; AC-027-05 is new.
- Whitelist: pi `agent_end`, `turn_end`, `tool_execution_end`, and toolResult `message_start`/`message_end`; claude `user`; codex App Server `turn/diff/updated`.
